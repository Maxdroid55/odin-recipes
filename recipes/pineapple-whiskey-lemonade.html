Pineapple Whiskey Lemonade

Refreshing and beautiful pineapple whiskey lemonade. For hot summer afternoons or nights.

Ingredients

  • 2 fluid ounces whiskey
  • 1 1/2 fluid ounces pineapple juice
  • 1 1/2 fluid ounces lemonade
  • 1/2 fluid ounce triple sec or simple syrup
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • ice, as needed

Steps

  1. Combine whiskey, pineapple juice, lemonade, triple sec, and lemon juice in a cocktail shaker. Fill cocktail shaker with ice; cover and shake until the outside of shaker has frosted.
  2. Strain into a chilled glass or a glass with ice.
